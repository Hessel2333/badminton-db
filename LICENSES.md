Attribute original metadata curation to **BadmintonDB contributors**, link to
<https://github.com/Hessel2333/badminton-db>, name the release you used, link to
CC BY 4.0, and indicate your changes. This applies to our contributions only;
it does not grant rights in manufacturers' photographs, packaging graphics,
trademarks or other third-party content, or assert exclusive rights in facts.

The initial release includes **metadata about 69 images and zero image binaries**.
Those image records are `rights.status: unreviewed` and
`distribution.status: withheld`. A SHA-256, filename or processing history is
provenance, not a redistribution permission.

Generated or reconstructed product images must retain their processing history
and be identified as such. AI generation, background extraction and tracing do
not constitute a claim that third-party artwork has been cleared for reuse.
The VICTOR render records' fixed barcode `1234567890128` is an illustration;
it is not a verified GTIN or manufacturer SKU and must not be used for identification.

To distribute an image, set an explicit open license, rights holder and evidence
URL, have the permission reviewed, and add the file under `media/files/` with its
checksum. The validator rejects a public image without approved rights, an
unreferenced image binary, a checksum mismatch, or an unsafe file path.

Brand and product names identify the relevant equipment. BadmintonDB is an
independent community project and does not imply manufacturer endorsement.
